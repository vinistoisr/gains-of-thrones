Dashboard

People

Each person needs a source of sleep data: an Ultrahuman token, or an Oura or Google Health sign-in. A workout log is optional.

Sign-in apps

Oura and Google Health only let apps read your data through a sign-in, so this dashboard needs its own free app registration with each one you use. You do this once; after that every person connects with a button.

Refresh

Data is fetched every 6 hours. After adding a person, refresh once to build the dashboard.

Time zone and push

Dates, bedtimes and the 07:00 push use this time zone.
